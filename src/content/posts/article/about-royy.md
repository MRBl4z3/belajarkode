## Hi, I'm Royy
 
I'm a Full Stack Developer from Balikpapan, Indonesia. I build websites and tools with React, Node.js, and Python, and I write programming learning materials so more people can get started with coding.
 
## What I Work On
 
- **Web development:** building websites and web apps with React and Node.js.
- **Python:** from small programs to teaching materials for beginners.
- **Learning materials:** tutorials and course content for programming, including Python.
## Projects
 
- **RetroLens:** a hand-tracking camera filter app built with Python, OpenCV, and MediaPipe.
- **Belajar Kode:** the site you are reading right now, a growing collection of learning resources for developers.
## Why Belajar Kode
 
Belajar Kode is where I collect and publish the tutorials and resources I write, with the goal of making it easier for people to learn programming.
